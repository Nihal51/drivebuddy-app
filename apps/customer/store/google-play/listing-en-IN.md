Need someone to drive your car? DriveBuddy sends a police-verified, sober driver to your door, usually in about 30 minutes, any time of day or night. You keep your own car; we bring the driver.

DriveBuddy serves Raipur, Naya Raipur (Atal Nagar), Bhilai, Durg and Bilaspur, and nearby towns such as Kumhari, Arang, Abhanpur and Tilda-Neora.

WHAT YOU CAN BOOK
• By the hour: 1 hour for a meeting, an errand or a quick hospital visit; 3 hours for shopping, family visits or an airport run

• Night driver, for late returns from parties and functions
• Monthly driver: your own regular driver, with Basic and Premium plans

CLEAR PRICES
The app shows our current prices before you book, for example ₹300 for 1 hour, ₹600 for 3 hours and ₹1,000–1,200 for a full day. There is no payment in the app: we call you to confirm the price before the driver leaves.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose a service, your city and your pickup address.
2. We call you back within minutes to confirm.
3. A verified driver arrives and drives your car.

WHY PEOPLE CHOOSE DRIVEBUDDY
• Police-verified drivers with 5+ years of experience

• Usually at your door in about 30 minutes, 24/7
• Safe for women, families and senior citizens
• A local Chhattisgarh team that answers the phone at any hour

IN THE APP
• Book in under a minute, with or without logging in

We ask only for what we need to send you a driver. The app does not use your location, contacts or photos, and shows no ads. You can delete your account from the app at any time.
